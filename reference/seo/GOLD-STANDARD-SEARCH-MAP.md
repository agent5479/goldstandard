## Priority summary

1. **Rank first:** dog training Golden Bay (home) + problem commercial roots (leash, recall, reactive, puppy, behaviour, difficult).
2. **Mention heavily:** symptom language (pulling, lunging, barking, jumping, settle, recall failure).
3. **Gloss, don’t SEO-chase:** proprietary Gold Standard vocabulary.
4. **Separate cluster:** breed tools — never compete with local commercial titles.
5. **Ignore:** positive-only rebrand and thin geo doorways.
